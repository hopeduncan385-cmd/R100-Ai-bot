How the analyzer works

MATCH: frequency of the chosen digit.
DIFFER: frequency of all other digits.
OVER / UNDER: frequency above or below the selected digit.
EVEN / ODD: frequency of even or odd digits.
RISE / FALL: recent upward or downward tick movements.

This is a statistical analysis tool, not a trained AI prediction model. A high historical frequency does not prove that a contract has a future advantage. Compare actual payout and losses before making any decision. No trades are placed by this application.
